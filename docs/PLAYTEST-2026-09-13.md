## The author's rules — said while playing, they bind every item below

1. **A screen never loses its scroll position.** Whatever re-renders it — a tap on a card, a
   journal line, a timer — the player stays exactly where they were. Not «restore it
   afterwards»: nothing may knock it off in the first place. One global fix, not per screen.
2. **Every tab and screen answers «чтобы что?»** — *why is this here for me, and what will I do
   here?* What do we show the player, so that he does what? A screen that only shows (a log, a
   dial, a list to read) fails until it names the deed it leads to. Run every tab of the table
   and the station through this before redesigning any of them.
3. **Optimise without losing quality — only improve.** «Смотрится хорошо.» No cutting effects,
   detail or resolution to buy frames; the win comes from doing the same work cheaper.
4. **The ship stays under the finger.** While a finger steers or rests on the ship, the view does
   not drift and the ship does not slide away.

And the umbrella direction: **rework all the interfaces** — think through where everything sits
from the point of view of convenience: actions, buttons, hints. The findings below are the
concrete cases one hour of play ran into.

## 1. Interface bugs

### 1.1 Scroll jumps to the top — ОПИСЬ, «Смена», АЛЬБОМ, ПРИЁМНИКИ, all of the table
The author: on ОПИСЬ a tap on a card («Аварийная пусковая установка») scrolls the list up, and
it also «scrolls up by itself, periodically». Later: «в альбоме та же беда со скролом, это
глобально надо лечить».

- **Why:** the table pages rebuild by emptying their box and filling it again — `opisRender`
  (`27j-ui-opis`) starts with `box.textContent=""`, `renderSmena` (`12ud-smena`) and the album
  (`25g-postcard`) with `box.innerHTML=""`, `renderRelays` (`11ap-relay`) the same. For a moment
  `#tableBody` is empty, the browser clamps `scrollTop` to 0, and nothing puts it back.
- **On a tap:** a card's click toggles the selection and calls `opisRerender()` → jump.
- **«By itself»:** every `logAdd` (`11-log`) calls `tableRender()` whenever the table is open —
  whatever page is open, not only the notebook. `recordAdd` (`11aa-record`) does the same. In
  flight lines come every few seconds (ether, market, pickets). **Measured live:** with ОПИСЬ
  scrolled to 448, the line «…на «Онэш» реголит богатый — разработку бы туда.» arrived at 25.4 s,
  and in the same 50 ms tick `scrollTop` went 448 → 0. With no taps and no journal lines, 15 s
  of watching showed zero DOM mutations and zero scroll changes.
- **Also by code:** the armed-confirm timeout on ОПИСЬ (`opisConfirm`) re-renders after 3.1 s.
- The station screens already keep their scroll (`26-ui-station`, `keep=same?$body.scrollTop:0`);
  the table never learned it. Rule 1 asks for more than restoring: the re-render itself must not
  disturb the position (diff in place, or keep the height while rebuilding), and a page that is
  not affected by a journal line must not re-render at all.

### 1.2 ОПИСЬ: an opened card does not scroll
The author: «прокрутка жёстко зависла», then «на открытой карточке прокрутка не работает».

- **Why:** `.opis .op-card.on{…touch-action:none}` (`style.css`). The selected card switches off
  every browser gesture so that the long-press lift (`opisDragWire` → 380 ms → `opisLift` with
  pointer capture) owns the finger. Unselected cards are `pan-y` and scroll fine.
- On the phone the opened card with its three buttons (ЗАМЕНИТЬ / РАЗОБРАТЬ / ОСТАВИТЬ) is about
  150 px tall — a dead zone in the middle of the list.
- Measured during the «hang»: the frame loop at 60 fps, no long tasks, `OPIS.drag` null, no
  ghost — the game was fine; only the touch-action held the finger.
- A trap next to it (by code, not seen): a journal line during a lift re-renders the list (1.1),
  the lifted card is detached, its `pointerup`/`pointercancel` never arrive, and
  `OPIS.drag` / `body.op-lift` can stay stuck.

### 1.3 ОПИСЬ tab strip looks cut off, ТРЮМ is shaded
The author: «меню обрезалось, трюм как-то затеняется».

- The strip КОРАБЛЬ · СНЯТОЕ · КОМПЛЕКТ · ТРЮМ (`nav.tabs.op-tabs`) is 288 px wide (x 26→314)
  inside a 387 px panel (x 12→399): the tabs do not stretch, the strip stops short of the edge.
- `scrollWidth == clientWidth` — nothing to scroll — yet the right-edge fade of `nav.tabs`
  (`style.css`, «the edge that doesn't end must look unfinished») is on, because the `tail` class
  that removes it is set only by `tabsSync()` (`15-input`), and the ОПИСЬ strip never passes
  through `tabsSync`. The last tab ТРЮМ (x 256→314) lies under the fade.

### 1.4 The compass chips climb to the thumb
The author confirmed from the 03:22 frame: «фишки компаса уезжают, и остальное верно».

- Frame: the thumb resting mid-screen (the stick ring reads «СТОП»; speed 0 — the stop does
  work when the thumb is truly still). The chips ЦИЦИИН · 1474 / ЗВЕЗДА · 2679 / НЕЙЭЛЬ I · 3426
  stand in a row across the middle of the screen; «ЗВЕЗДА» lies on the ship's tail.
- **Why:** the chips' bottom edge is lifted above the stick's footprint — in `drawSystem`
  (`17-mode-system`), `for(const f of feet)inY1=Math.min(inY1,(f.y-f.r)/U-10)` with
  `feet=helmStickFoot()`. Meant to keep chips from under the thumb; with the thumb in the middle
  the «edge» rides up to the middle and the chips jump wherever the finger lands.
- In the same frame: the picket «КОММУНА 731» wears a red health bar — it reads as an enemy, yet
  it cannot be locked (3.1). Fuel went 151 → 47 of 209 in 8 minutes of stick flying inside one
  system — worth a look at what a minute of thumb-flying costs.

## 2. Flight, camera, zoom

### 2.1 Judder and the frame (the author: «экран дёргается под пальцем»)
Two measurements: a 60 s in-page recorder of the author's flying (frame times, ship, stick),
then a 25 s Chrome trace.

- **No cadence on a 120 Hz screen.** Frame intervals land on 1, 2 or 3 vsyncs at random:
  histogram in 4.17 ms bins — 313 / 503 / 496 / 235 / 168 / 56 …; in the first recording long
  and short frames often alternated (13, 33, 15, 33 ms…). The world is integrated with a variable
  `dt` from the rAF time (`frameBody`, `28-loop`), so each displayed frame moves the world by a
  different amount — that is the judder. The camera itself is steady (camera-vs-ship offset p95
  1.8 px/frame): the whole world steps unevenly.
- In the first recording long frames (> 24 ms) were 23 % with a finger down against 8 % without,
  and active steering dropped to 44–46 fps. The trace did not confirm a finger cost in JS (the
  rAF callback is 9 ms median either way); the finger-time extra is in style/layout
  (`UpdateLayoutTree` 27 → 52 ms/s) and in the GPU work of whatever the thrust draws.
- **The nose turns in steps:** 0.08 rad per 60 Hz step (≈ 275°/s), 0.16 on long frames —
  160 frames in a minute with the nose jumping more than 4.6° in one frame.
- **Resolution stuck at ×1.** `resAuto` (`28-loop`) had dropped to `RES_AUTO=1` and never came
  back (`resUps` 0): the canvas was **411×742 on a DPR 2.625 screen — 1/7 of the native pixels**;
  the whole picture is soft. The way back needs 20 s of frames under 13 ms — never reached here.
- **The phone heats up:** after ~75 min thermal status 2 (MODERATE): skin 42 °C, SoC 53 °C,
  battery 32 → 40 °C. Right after the trace: **37 fps, median frame 33 ms — even at ×1.**
- What the frame costs is in §6.

### 2.2 Stopping (the author: «сложно остановиться»)
- 26 touches in a minute, mostly 0.2–0.9 s pokes — the author trying to stop.
- After release the ship coasts: typically 1–3 s and 100–1 000 units; the worst 15.3 s /
  2 273 units and 6.9 s / 2 229 units.
- The rule «a finger resting still = stop» (M422: dead zone `HELM_DEAD` 12 px, `HELM_TAKE_MS`
  420 ms) fired on **15 frames of 3 177**: a real thumb drifts past 12 px (stick offset change
  p90 6 px/frame), and pokes under 400 ms are read as taps, not the stick. Braking by pulling
  against the motion happened (256 retro frames, 38 toggles of the retro flag), but «stop here»
  has no gesture that works every time.

### 2.3 The ship slides from under the finger (rule 4)
The author: «держишь палец прям на корабле или управляешь прям на корабле — экран уезжает и
корабль из-под пальца уезжает. Вот это не надо.»

- **Why, by design:** `flightCam` (`16a-space`) lets the camera trail the ship — «the faster we
  fly, the more it trails; that reads as mass» (`k = .16 − speed·.012`) — plus a thrust shake.
- At zoom 0.17: the ship sits 5 px from the centre at rest, **median 28 / max 43 px at speed
  6–11**; during one touch it slid 10–64 px away from the thumb.

### 2.4 Zoomed in: the ship leaves the screen, and does not grow
- The lag is a fixed amount in *world* units, so on screen it grows with the zoom. **At zoom 2.38
  under the stick the ship sat 350–536 px from the centre** of a 411×742 screen — at or past the
  edge; at 0.16 the same speed gives 19–34 px (×14 difference).
- **The hull does not grow:** `shipScaleAt(Z)=clamp(Z, SHIP_SCALE_MIN .7, SHIP_SCALE_MAX .8)`
  (0.448.0). The world is drawn at 4.5 and the hull at 0.8 — 5.6× smaller than the world around
  it; at 0.16 it is 4.4× larger. Zooming in enlarges planets, stations and the wake, never your
  ship.
- At zoom 4.5 the first seconds ran 15–21 fps (gaps 87–109 ms), later 60 fps — recheck on a
  cool phone.

### 2.5 Pinch
- The range is 28× (`ZOOM_MIN` 0.16 – `ZOOM_MAX` 4.5) and the pinch maps finger distance
  straight to zoom: **217 frames changed the zoom by more than 6 % in one frame**; the author
  crossed the whole range in under a second (4.49 → 1.52 in 0.3 s) and spent about 20 s pinching
  back and forth, hunting a comfortable view and overshooting. No easing, no resting steps.
- After a pinch ends with one finger still down, the zoom drifts a little (8 frames, 1–3 %).

### 2.6 In orbit, zooming in loses the planet
The author: «при приближении на орбите планета исчезает» — the ringed gas giant Нейэль IV.

- Measured with `planetDraw` wrapped for 60 s: the ship holds orbit 225 units from the centre of
  a planet of radius 154. At zoom 4.5 the disc is 692 px in radius, its centre ~1 000 px from the
  ship, its surface ~320 px away — more than half of a 411 px portrait screen.
- The camera follows the ship, so as the ship goes round, the planet swings out of the frame
  (e.g. centre at 1165,825 px: nothing of it on screen; the renderer correctly skips it). What is
  left is a slice at an edge, or nothing — «in orbit» of empty space.
- The zoom has no idea of «keep the body I orbit in view». Same root as PLAN «2a. Seamless
  atmosphere entry» (the landing zone outside the ×4.5 frame) — one design for both.

### 4.3 АЛЬБОМ (`album`, `25g-postcard`) and postcards

## 5. «Смена» — from a list to the main quest

### 5.1 The text is invisible
Chapter 1 «Док» (03:30): the body text is pale blue on the cream sheet; only the gold drop-cap
reads. Measured: `<p>` colour `rgb(207,227,234)` from `.smena{…color:var(--text)}` — the light
colour of the dark screens; the table is now paper, so the contrast is about 1.1 : 1. `.cap` and
`hr.scene` use `var(--dim)` — same problem. The text also runs to the sheet's right edge
(x 40→399, sheet ends at ~399; `margin-left:28px`, no right margin).

### 5.2 A list, not a book; opened by money, out of order
- `renderSmena` (`12ud-smena`): a grey line, part headings, 72 rows (25 open); a tap unfolds the
  chapter *inline* under its row. An open chapter and a locked grey row look alike. No reader
  view, no page, no «next chapter», no place kept; the rebuild is `innerHTML=""` (1.1).
- The author: «я даже планеты не посещал, просто дронов купил — пол-книги открылось».
  **Measured on his save: 25 open — 1–5, 10, 11, 19, 20, 26, 29, 33–35, 46–48, 50–52, 55, 59, 67,
  70, 71.** The triggers that fired are money, holding and fleet moments («первая машина на
  точке», «первый наёмник», «аппетит узла и наряд», «свой флагман», «ультиматум», «трудовая
  книжка», «комиссия», «сто первая»…), reachable without a single landing. The end is readable
  («последний рейс» 59, «комиссия» 70, «сто первая» 71) while 6–9 are shut.
- The unlock places (`SMENA_CH`, 72, all distinct): many are events or menu moments — «первая
  честная продажа и дом», «первая зарубка отчёта», «первая/вторая глава отчёта прочитана»,
  «первый наёмник», «пять слов отчёта», «другое взросление» — a snapshot there is a dock or a
  screen. Some are scenery already: «луг, который светится», «роща», «медленная долина», «три
  света», «рукав, который гаснет», «зеркало эфира».

### 5.3 Direction (the author)
- **«Смена» is the main quest, and a hard one.** A chapter is a milestone. Editing the book's text
  to fit is allowed.
- **Closing a chapter must be felt** — a big on-screen moment, «like АКТ 2», an effect across the
  screen. The named reference is **No Man's Sky** — its main storyline is the model («квест там
  прикольный основной»); other mechanics may be borrowed from it too.
- **A real book on the tab:** comfortable, absorbing reading for someone who gets hooked; with
  pictures, references and photos — pages that open **with images from your own game** at the
  milestone that unlocked them (the postcard painter from a snapshot is already there, 4.3).

## 6. Performance — what the frame costs on the phone (Chrome trace, 25 s)

Rule 3 applies: same look or better, cheaper work.

- **JS is not the bottleneck.** The rAF callback: median 9 ms, p90 15.6 ms, the same with and
  without a finger; the main thread is idle 60–73 % of the time.
- **The GPU process is the busiest thread — canvas raster.** `RasterDecoderImpl::DoEndRasterCHROMIUM`:
  12 s of GPU time in the capture, p90 5.4 ms per raster, max 17 ms. «The frame eats raster, not
  JS» holds on the phone too.
- **No cadence** (2.1): intervals over 8–25 ms on a 120 Hz display. The longest gaps (38–65 ms)
  carry 9–40 ms of GPU raster, twice a Major GC of 14–28 ms.
- **Own JS by self time (ms per second of wall):** `drawImage` 49–65, **`drawWake` 31–56** (the
  0.449 wake — the dearest game function), `stroke` 21–32, `hud` 18–25, `drawTrail` 19–22,
  **`getBoundingClientRect` 10–15** (layout reads every frame), `drawHull` 9–11,
  `querySelectorAll` 4–5 (DOM queries every frame), `drawSystem` 6–10, `drawStars` 4–5.
  Style and layout: 2 096 Layout/UpdateLayoutTree events, 937 ms in total.
- **Sound: a quarter of a core, always** — the «Reverb convolution background» thread busy about
  250 ms of every second (the convolution reverb).
- **Resolution** fell to ×1 and stayed (2.1).
- A single 205 ms `pointermove` in the capture is V8 interrupt callbacks — the tracer starting,
  not the game.

Where the cheaper-same-look work lies: a steady frame cadence (pace to a vsync multiple and step
the world by it); bake what does not change between frames (the «painted once» rule); the wake
and the HUD drawn cheaper at the same look; no layout reads and DOM queries per frame; the
reverb's cost; the resolution climbing back instead of sticking at ×1.

## Tools — how the measurements were taken
All in `docs/night-2026-09-13/raw/phone-tools/` (stdlib Python + PIL; `CDP_PORT` 9333):
- `adb reverse tcp:8812 tcp:8812` + `python -m http.server 8812` serves a local build to the
  phone at `127.0.0.1:8812` — no deploy, no shared pools.
- `cdp.py eval|evalfile|tapjs|hold|shot` — the game tab only (`127.0.0.1:8812` or
  `drift-game.ru`), never other tabs. `tapjs` evaluates a position and taps it with a real touch.
- `ph.py shot OUT.png SCALE` — `adb exec-out screencap`, downscaled.
- `st.js` — one-line state probe (mode, ship, fuel, prompt, hail, windows, buttons, chips, log).
- Recorders run inside the page on rAF into `window.__rec/__z/__pl` and are pulled afterwards
  (`rec.py`, `z1.py`, `pl.py`); a long CDP await times out at 60 s, so never await a recorder.
- `trace.py OUT.json SECONDS` — `Tracing.start` with the timeline, cpu-profiler, cc/viz/gpu
  categories, streamed back (25 s ≈ 180 MB); `tran.py`/`tran2.py`/`tran3.py` split it by thread,
  by event kind, by JS function and by finger-down time, and list the longest frame gaps.
- Thermal: `adb shell dumpsys thermalservice`, `dumpsys battery`.
