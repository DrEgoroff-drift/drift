## 1. Remember Tomorrow («Помни будущее», SoftWarWare, St Petersburg, DOS 1997–98) and Polaris Sector (2016)

**What it is.** A real-time space 4X. Premise: on 31.12.1999 two alien fleets destroy each other
over Earth; from the wreckage humanity gets to the stars — the plot never touches play. Seven
playable races (люди, магелланцы, вагаларрианцы, эрианцы, шаратары, гавакены…), three non-playable
(логаны, дриллы, урганы); **races differ in look and lore, not in numbers** — except the Gavaken,
who cannot build a dreadnought and have the best fighters (the one-trait rule we took for the yards).
Nine scenarios («Дизъюнкция», «Король на холме», «Пептид», «Битва за Сириус», «Блиц»…).
Игромания 07.1998: 7/10; Old-Games.ru 8/10, players 9.56/10 — «душевнейшая и уютная игра»; crashes
(patched), the rest a bit repetitive. Re-released by the authors: v1.01 (XP via DOSBox, scenario
generator), v1.04 (native Windows/macOS), v1.5 (new graphics); remade as Polaris Sector
(Slitherine; SpaceSector 5.5/10, Explorminate «recommended»).

**Map.** Stars joined by **subspace tunnels with real length**: a tunnel costs time and fuel;
scouting, the way back and control of the forks are what matter (Игромания's guide: expand by
neighbouring chains, or jump 2–3 stars ahead to hold the forks and block rivals).
**Economy.** Бурмитий — the strategic mineral for flight and combat; риидиум for ships and energy;
plutonium, metals, organics, food. Shipping a resource between planets costs 20 % of it.
Colonies by hand or on automatic profiles. **Science.** A slider between фундаментальная (opens
branches) and прикладная (gives things); nothing peaceful is ever invented.
**The designer** (what the author likes). A hull is a volume on **up to five decks**; the player
places every component — engines, shields, reactors, scanners, tanks, colony module, fighter
bay, cloak, anti-gravity — limited only by compartment volume. **Top deck: weapons with a 360°
arc; inner decks: ~90°.** Amount and kind of equipment set speed, manoeuvrability and energy
draw. Classes: fighters (only in hangars for interstellar flight), corvettes (first independent
hulls: combat, colony, landing), cruisers (light/heavy/line — the heavy one is the workhorse),
dreadnoughts, satellites, platforms. Favourite trick: an anti-gravity drive on a battle station.
Polaris Sector formalised it: energy per area (a medium gravitational generator ≈ 244 energy per
area unit), mass → speed, hardpoints labelled by arc (top 360° / side restricted), a Fleet
Designer with pre-battle formation. **Combat.** Real time, hundreds of ships; the player places
forces first; the field wraps around; the guide's formation — anti-fighter line, then stations,
then the main body. Diplomacy simple; no espionage.
**Loved:** the hull is engineered — where a gun sits decides what it can hit. **Hated:** redesigning
scouts and colony ships every game; re-fitting the roster by hand after each technology (players
asked for the AI's auto-designer).

## 2. Starsector — the refit screen

**Hull = fixed chassis:** sizes frigate/destroyer/cruiser/capital (+ civilian variants); fixed per
hull: mounts (position, size, type, arc), built-in hullmods, the unique **ship system** (one
active ability — Burn Drive, Plasma Jet, Fast Missile Racks…), base flux, fighter bays, logistics,
and **one ordnance-point budget nothing can raise** (≈50 / 100 / 150 / 300–400).
**Mounts:** small/medium/large; ballistic, energy, missile, and hybrid (ball+energy), composite
(ball+missile), synergy (energy+missile), universal, built-in; a weapon one size smaller of the
same type fits, larger never; **turret** (wide arc, fragile) vs **hardpoint** (forward arc, ~2×
durable); arcs drawn as cones in the UI.
**OP** buys weapons, wings, hullmods, **flux vents** (+10 dissipation / 1 OP) and **capacitors**
(+200 capacity / 1 OP), capped per size (10/20/30/50). The closed budget is the puzzle.
**Flux:** capacity + dissipation; **soft flux** (firing) dissipates always, **hard flux** (shield
hits) only with shields down; over capacity while shielded = **overload** (nothing works,
dissipation halved, 4–10 s+); **vent** = 2× dissipation, totally vulnerable. (We decided one
energy bar, no venting — war §4; kept.)
**Hullmods:** OP-costed passives; logistics mods capped at 2; **built-in** free; **S-mods** — a
story point welds a mod in, refunds its OP, green bars on the icon (our доводка); **D-mods** —
damage mods on recovered wrecks, cheaper to run, removable for money (our шрамы).
**Groups:** up to 7, linked/alternating, autofire per group (war §3.3 has three). **Officers** layer
skills on hulls (our managers/crew). **Variants** = saved loadouts, faction variants; **autofit**
fills from stock, flagged as suboptimal (our ТИПОВОЙ / ПРОЕКТЫ).
**Sourcing:** markets, blueprints, salvage, faction exclusives; **tech levels** — low-tech (armour,
ballistics, blocky: Hegemony, Luddic Church), midline (League, Diktat), high-tech (energy, shields,
sleek: Tri-Tachyon); pirates scavenged, Remnant drone-AI. Faction = tech level + palette + weapon
lean — an enemy fleet is read before any label (our maker grammar does the palette; the yards'
character adds the lean).
**Reception:** praised for depth and combat (RPS, Kotaku — a Star Control II successor);
criticised for balance and the manual tuning time — which is also what players call the fun.

## 3. Faction presence without a label (Part C)

Starsector: transponder on/off, owned relays and buoys, patrol composition and aggression,
liveries, market ownership. EVE: security status colour, CONCORD, sovereignty, live gate-camp
status. Elite: the controlling faction owns the starport, system states change boards and patrols.
X4: the sector's owner runs police scans, flags contraband, charges fees — the police *is* the
border. Freelancer: house architecture and culture (Liberty Art Deco, Bretonia, Kusari, Rheinland),
radio chatter, ship silhouettes. Mass Effect: the Citadel's Presidium vs Wards, Omega as the
neon-noir opposite. **The channels:** silhouette + paint, station architecture, patrol behaviour,
transponder, customs scans, relay ownership, radio tone, music, lighting, laws/contraband, prices,
border buoys. → borders §0–2; the review made the *first ship's gesture* the lead channel.

## 4. Cinema — how space is made inhabited

Guardians (Knowhere — a mining town in a dead giant's head; Contraxia — a neon truck stop; the
Ravagers' junk fleet; the Walkman as anachronism), The Fifth Element (stacked traffic lanes with
police, the flying fast-food junk, Fhloston Paradise — a liner-hotel, Ruby Rhod's broadcast),
Valerian (Alpha grown by accretion; the Big Market), Blade Runner (advertising at monstrous scale,
neon in many scripts, rain), Star Wars (Coruscant lanes, the cantina, Canto Bight), DS9's
Promenade (market + customs + square), Cowboy Bebop (Mars neon, ships stacking on arrival),
Futurama (ads everywhere, played straight), and ours — Кин-дза-дза! (КЦ as money, «ку», the
пепелац, caste satire), Тайна третьей планеты (the space zoo's errand to the animal market on Блук,
Шелезяка), Гостья из будущего (the spaceport as a railway station). **Devices:** traffic lanes with
lights · advertising at scale · a hotel/resort as destination · food sold in space · junk ships ·
customs/police · crowds around one big thing · broadcast chatter · signs in many scripts · one
giant as scale cue · **institutions that work** (the finding that decided life §1) · light pollution.

## 5. Gates, lanes, metro

Freelancer trade lanes (rings, ride at speed, pirates cut a lane; jump gates vs wild jump holes),
X Rebirth/X4 highways (join/leave anywhere; large hulls fly outside), EVE gates (the political
map; gate camps), Mass Effect relays (primary fixed pairs, secondary flexible), Babylon 5 (gates
and improvised jump points; the Zocalo), Bebop (toll + destination announced; the gate accident
that wrecked the Moon), The Expanse (the Ring's slow zone, Medina as garrison), Stargate (dialing
as performance), Stellaris (hyperlanes + gateways + L-gates through one hub), No Man's Sky (glyph
portals; the Anomaly as a lobby). **Nobody does stops along a line you may leave early** — ours.
What makes it fun: the ride is a scene; waiting has a reason; stops in between; disruptions on the
scheme; visible danger; a Beck-logic scheme. Moscow: palaces per station, «Осторожно, двери
закрываются. Следующая станция…», жетоны, the scheme on the wall, the ring line; the электричка
that stops everywhere vs the скорый at junctions; the полустанок with one lamp.

## 6. «Кибердеревня» (Кинопоиск 2023; s2 autumn 2025; Сергей Васильев, Birchpunk)

Farmer Николай Кулибин on Mars at «Рязань Каунти» with tractor Митя, android Глаша, flying van
Буля; the corporation «Ижевск Дайнемикс»; the oligarch's mind in a cleaning robot; the hologram
wife on subscription who stages a coup; each planet a Russian region (a bazaar on Saturn's rings,
Церерск, Юпитербург, a sanctioned planet where «у нас всё хорошо»); Космопочта open four hours a
week; grandmothers on jet shopping carts; QR codes on cows. Кинопоиск ~8/10 (2nd most watched of
2023), IMDb ~7.5; «Мир фантастики» series of the year. **Praised:** the collision, the VFX, the
**warmth** («тёплый, почти домашний киберпанк»; Кулибин as «русский Тед Лассо»). **Criticised:**
thin dramaturgy, too little village; s2 dropped the mockumentary frame and lost charm. **The
devices:** village life persisting in high tech; tech held with изолента; everything by subscription
(the fridge withholds food); the hologram on a tariff; hybrid names; a region per planet; the fake
newscast; machines with names; the post's hours; life going on; warmth. What we refuse: open
references, real brands, the road-trip structure.

## 7. Our code on 0.449.0 — what the designs build on

**Ship.** `SHIPS` 8 hand-built hulls (`thr turn fuel cargo hull`), `FLEET` 100 by seed in six tiers,
unique and fused hulls; `HULL_CLASS` 7 templates; `HULL_MAKER` six form grammars (eight dimensions:
profile law, frame, protrusions, seam, finish, marks, engine signature, sound) — look only;
`HULL_ROLE` six professions derived from the hull. `slotsOf` 4–8 categorical slots + missile;
`slotAnchors` real geometry; `mountsOf` size L/M/H by hull mass (wing tips a step lighter),
**жёсткая** on the axis (cone ×.5, dmg ×1.25) / **турель** on the sides; parts `{s,t,k,g,i}` seven
kinds, affixes, tiers 1–5, `.cap`; `capOf` 30–48 one budget for modules and parts; `MODS` seven
station upgrades; `GUN_FAMILY` eleven (twenty in war §2.1); nodes (ten families × five grades →
crowns); clearance I–IV gates tiers. One energy bar (war §4), three shield behaviours, hit location
×1.6/×.7. Fitted guns, shields and engine colour are drawn. Screens: ОПИСЬ (four zones, phone tabs,
`opisShipFuture` deltas), ОСНАСТКА at the dock, стрельбище (`24d-range`). Player owns several
hulls (`G.fleet`). **Gaps:** no plan/volume, no trade hold-vs-guns, no maker character in play, no
hull history, no fast path.
**Powers.** `POWERS` six with hail/air/never/food/paper/suit/voice; `chronOwner(sx,sy)` from the
chronicle; picket 2–3 at the jump point (rear) or a battle (front); hail once per 30 min within
900 px; station header names the power, red «FLAG CHANGED» for 8 сводки, prices ×.7 under
occupation; station body takes one tint from `station.by`; map emblem chip ≤ 7.5 px, red dashed
front; six radio waves; Ялта at r≈6 with sealed weapons. **Gaps:** nothing in the first five seconds
of a jump; traffic ignores the owner; music ignores the power; posts stand still.
**Travel.** `jump()` (18-mode-map): fuel 9 + 13·d, instant, the ship placed at 1 500 from the star at
a random angle; `stat().jump` 3–7 sectors. Rescue tow when dry (`16c-rescue`, HAUL 5 min). The
трасса exists only as map topology (`18b-map-hold`). System view draws nebula, stars, planets,
one station body, belt rocks, shuttles on arcs by rung, planet lights and works; **no signage,
neon, hotels, queues or gates**. Static raster via `18c-chunks` (`screenLayer`, `chunkStore`),
≤ 4 ms/frame bake budget. Galaxy: barred spiral around 0:0, `CHRON_R` 10, danger to r=40, M447–M451
queued. **Resources.** Ten trade goods (7–190), four rare raws with named eaters, the industrial
chain, missiles, people; deposits by `PROFILE` and `BELT_RES`; distance changes tiers, not what is
in the ground; later goods take their own random salt.
