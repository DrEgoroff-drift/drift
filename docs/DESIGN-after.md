# After the act — the institute, the trainee, the joys (M162–M166)

Design, 2026-08-23. Five milestones for after the second act (`docs/DESIGN-act2.md`). Two are
systems (the institute, the trainee), three are "joys" to be slotted between big milestones when a
breather is needed (zoo, wall paper, dominoes). All in the Soviet key: the institute of the
Strugatskys, the trainee of "Trainees" and "Guest from the Future", the zoo of Bulychev, the wall
newspaper and the request concert of any station's common room.

## M162 — the institute as a living client (Strugatsky)

- The plan (`11r-plan`) is numbers. The institute is **people with topics**: «тема 7-Б, плывущие
  часы, руководитель Привалов». Topics come as letters to the counter; reports are handed as
  tapes; forms («справка по форме 3-Б»), «зайдите через неделю».
- Bureaucratic comedy: two labs order the same tape; your tape goes «не в тот отдел» and
  surfaces a month later with a reprimand in the record book (M161); a topic is closed «за
  неактуальностью» the day you deliver.
- **The voucher** («путёвка»): a completed topic earns a voucher to the sanatorium planet (one
  per region with a resort settlement): the crew rests, morale is restored, Vega (M153) is
  delighted, the parrot gets a tan line. A rest is a landing with nothing to do for three days.
- Data: `11ab-institute` (topics, labs, forms, vouchers), lines in `12k-institute`. Suite `91zzq`.

## M163 — the trainee aboard ("Trainees", "Guest from the Future")

- The opposite of Vega: a **stowaway boy** found in the hold after a fair, who wants to be a
  cosmonaut. Uses Vega's infrastructure (right seat, HUD line, speech queue) — the two cannot be
  aboard at once; Vega stays home those days and says so.
- He learns in stages: touches instruments (a socket knocked, a line on the panel); reads the
  charts (the free "nearest X" like Vega); asks to be sent as a hire on a run; gets his
  «диплом» from the institute and leaves on his own hull. A year later his voice is on the
  ether with your call sign in it.
- Data: `11ac-trainee` (`G.trainee`: stage, days), lines in `12k-trainee`. Suite `91zzr`.

## M164 — the space zoo (Bulychev) — a joy

- Fauna is scanned and drawn (`20-life`); the parrot is Govorun. **Bring a beast home**: a
  scanned beast within reach can be caught (one cargo slot, it complains); at home it lives in
  the garage, then in a «живой угол» with pens on the living tier.
- Beasts need feed from what you carry, talk to each other, escape (found in the study). Vega
  is against. One region has a **zoo station** that takes beasts as cargo — «Тайна третьей
  планеты» whole.
- Data: `11ad-zoo`, pens in `27e-ui-home`. Suite `91zzs`.

## M165 — the wall newspaper and the request concert — a joy

- **Wall newspaper** («стенгазета») on the counter wall: a caricature of whoever delayed the
  barge (it may be you), a «молния» about a plan overfulfilled, a poem by a keeper. Drawn as a
  sheet with three cells; text from the news (`12p`) and the record book (M161).
- **Request concert** on the receiver: the player sends a greeting to a station; it sounds in
  their ether (a three-note call sign from the generative music, M42, plus the line); Vega
  listens; a rival answers with his own. Data: `11ae-concert`. Suite `91zzt`.

## M166 — dominoes at the table — a joy

- The table is a channel. In the cantina and at home: **dominoes** (and chess with the sixth
  rival only) against the crew, rivals, Vega. A tiny game, three turns a round, the stake a
  rumour or a part. Losing to Vega is a quarrel; winning is a quarrel.
- Data: `11af-domino`, drawn on the table layer. Suite `91zzu`.
