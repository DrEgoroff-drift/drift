# Берёзопанк — the satire and the mechanics of «Кибердеревня» (2026-09-14)

The author, 14.09.2026: «Кибердеревня — изучи всё, нам нужен такой же стёб и механики». The
research, what fits our tone law, ten devices turned into mechanics, and the queue Д1–Д10; nothing
is built yet.

## 0. The show, in one screen

«Кибердеревня» (Кинопоиск, 2023; season 2 in autumn 2025) — Сергей Васильев's series, grown out of
his 2020 Birchpunk short «Русская кибердеревня» (robots milking cows, a flying УАЗ «буханка», 13 M
views). Head writer Никита Ворожищев. Around 2085–2100 the farmer **Николай Кулибин** lives on Mars
with his family at a homestead called **«Рязань Каунти»**, with a tractor with a personality
(**Митя**), an android milkmaid (**Глаша**) and a flying van (**Буля**). His old partner
**Барагозин**, now head of the corporation **«Ижевск Дайнемикс»**, arrives to bulldoze the village;
a mind-transfer accident puts the oligarch into a battered cleaning robot, and the two cross the
solar system to Earth to swap him back. The villain turns out to be **Галя**, a hologram wife on
subscription, who runs a corporate coup through her owner. Each planet on the way is a Russian
region in space: a bazaar on Saturn's rings, a merchant town on Ceres, «Юпитербург», a sanctioned
planet where everyone insists that *they* are fine and it is the others who suffer. Space-post
open four hours a week; grandmothers flying on shopping carts with jet engines; QR codes on cows.

**Reception:** ~8/10 on Кинопоиск (the platform's second most watched series of 2023), ~7.5 on
IMDb; «Мир фантастики»'s series of the year. **Praised:** the collision itself, the best VFX in
Russian TV, and above all the **warmth** — «тёплый, почти домашний киберпанк», a hero who refuses
to profit off others (a reviewer called Кулибин «русский Тед Лассо»). **Criticised:** thin
dramaturgy, gags over plot, **too little of the village itself** (most of the season is the road
trip). Season 2 dropped the mockumentary frame for straight drama and lost much of its charm.

## 1. The devices, and our tone law

Our law is DESIGN-base §40: **the absurdity is always the world's own logic taken seriously —
never a joke from outside the world**; no memes, no character who knows they are funny; the joke
never eats clarity (§22). And from §7.1: satire on the state and the firm, never on people.

«Кибердеревня» passes that law because its central device is *a fact of its world*, not a wink:
**the future was built by the same people with the same изолента**. «Birchpunk» — a cutting-edge
concept, a provincial execution. That is exactly our naming register Б+А: «Красный уголок» and
«Столовая» at the edge of the galaxy, the seam Soviet science fiction was made of. So we take:

| device in the show | why it works | passes our law when |
|---|---|---|
| **village life persisting in high tech** (баня, огород, дача, трактор, соседи) | the familiar makes the strange lovable | it is how people in the world really live, never a reference |
| **tech held with изолента**, running on old hardware | poverty and ingenuity; a hero who fixes | the fix works — badly, visibly, for real |
| **everything by subscription** (the fridge withholds food) | the sharpest satire of the firm | the price and the lapse are stated on paper |
| **the hologram wife on a tariff** | monetisation as a character | she is a product, and the product is consistent |
| **hybrid names** — «Рязань Каунти», «Ижевск Дайнемикс» | a whole economy in two words | the names follow a rule of who owns what |
| **a region per planet** | geography as satire | each power's land has one signature place |
| **the fake newscast** | the broadcast contradicts what you saw | the contradiction is real, and the player saw it |
| **machines with names and quirks** (Митя, Глаша, Буля) | the cheapest affection in the show | the quirk has an effect |
| **the post open four hours a week** | the institution as weather | the hours are real and shown |
| **grandmothers on jet carts** | life going on, stubborn and funny | they have somewhere to go |
| **warmth** | the show's single biggest lesson | every satirical system has one earnest beat (§3) |

What we do **not** take: references worn openly (Blade Runner, Terminator — our law forbids jokes
from outside the world); real brands and places as brands (УАЗ, Почта России, РЖД — we invent);
the road-trip structure (we are already a road; the show's critics wanted *more village*, and our
village is the base).

## 2. Ten devices, ten mechanics

### Д1 Machines with names — «Митя встал, чинится сам, ругается»
Drones, the base's tractor-crawler, the tug, the barge's autopilot get **a name and one quirk**
from a small table (Митя, Глаша, Буля, Кузя, Жучка, Громобой…; quirks: «встаёт по понедельникам»,
«возит лишнее», «боится темноты — работает только днём», «поёт при бурении»). The quirk is one
number off the norm, both ways (the one that works only by day works faster by day). The journal's
drone lines speak in their name: today `crash.log` is full of «Дрон Д-… встал · чинится сам» —
tomorrow it is «Митя встал. Чинится сам. Ругается». Drones still never die (decided 2026-09-03).
Hired hands stay faceless bets; only *machines* get names — the joke is that the machines are the
people here.

### Д2 Изолента — the fix that works badly
A consumable, sold everywhere for kopecks: **repairs any part or the hull to 50 % in the field**
(the first hour's ДО 50 % button, available anywhere, one roll per repair). It **leaves a mark**:
a strip of tape drawn on the hull where it was used — a scar in the shipyard's sense (§6 there),
cleared only by a real repair at a yard. A hired hand or a manager can carry the trait
**«кулибин»**: fixes with tape and scrap at no cost, and the repairs hold at 60 %. Рассвет's yard
treats tape as a finish, not a scar.

### Д3 Подписка — the firm's best product
Компания and Хай-Фронт sell top parts and base modules **by subscription**: a tenth of the price
now, a fee per сводка. When the subscription lapses, the thing goes grey — at a сводка boundary,
announced a shift ahead in ПОЧТА (the §22 guard: it is never unclear). If it lapses in a fight, the
pad offers **«ЭКСТРЕННОЕ ПРОДЛЕНИЕ · ×3»** — one tap, the joke and the fairness at once. A base
module on subscription (the fridge of the show — the base's cold store) stops giving food, not
taking it. Owning is always possible and always dearer up front.

### Д4 Голограмма на тарифе — the AI core as a product
The manager rules already say an AI core, «once it exists», takes one of the four seats. It is a
**Хай-Фронт product with tariffs**: **БАЗОВЫЙ** (free — works, and reads an advert into every third
report), **ПРЕМИУМ** (useful: route prices, a real forecast), **СЕМЕЙНЫЙ** (as premium, and it
starts offering its opinion on how you live). Its loyalty is its subscription: unpaid, it does not
leave — it downgrades itself and apologises. She is a product, consistently, and never a romance.
The fifth-seat rule holds: she takes a seat like everyone.

### Д5 «Рязань Каунти» — names by who owns what
A name rule for settlements, holdings and metro stops: **a homely toponym + the owner's
administrative suffix**. ГЛАВТРАССА «посёлок городского типа Верхний Пояс»; Компания «Горловина
Каунти», «Нейэль-Сити»; Орднунг «Бецирк Нейэль № 4»; Коммуна «Сен-Горловина»; Рассвет «кооператив
«Горловина»»; Хай-Фронт «Горловина-2 v3.1». When the flag changes, the sign is repainted and the
suffix changes with it — the name tells you whose it is (a borders channel for free). Firms are
named the same way: a provincial city + a foreign tech word, invented («Кострома Роботикс»,
«Урюпинск Орбитал») — never a real company.

### Д6 One region-place per power (with life Ж6)
Every power's land gets one signature place that is *its* stereotype in space, played as the
power's own pride: ГЛАВТРАССА's «Дом водителя» the size of a station; the Компания's bazaar on a
ring of rocks; Орднунг's customs city where every building is a form; the Коммуна's dry dock where
one beautiful hull has been built for three hundred years; Рассвет's belt town cut into the rocks;
Хай-Фронт's relay garden. The life document's giants are these places.

### Д7 The newscast that contradicts you
The six waves already never say certain things (war §7.3). Now the retelling (`12p-news`) answers
**the player's own last deed** within one сводка, in each power's doublespeak: you pulled a barge
out of a fight — Маяк: «на трассе спокойно»; Компания: «партнёр обеспечил безопасность
перевозок™»; Хай-Фронт: «инцидент не зафиксирован». The player is the only one who knows the truth,
and the game never says so. This is the enrichment the phone playtest's P12 (ЭФИР) asks for.

### Д8 Космопочта — the institution as weather
ГЛАВТРАССА's post at stations is **open by the game clock** (a few hours, shown on the door).
Parcels arrive as **извещения** in ПОЧТА: a hull ordered on СТАПЕЛЬ (shipyard К6), a rare part,
the co-operative's goods. You collect at a post counter in opening hours; a parcel waits 30 days,
then goes back to the sender. The queue has a number; the clerk is kind («полежит ещё денёк, не по
правилам»). Satire on the institution, warmth in the person.

### Д9 Дачники — life going on
By the real calendar (the holiday module, `11am`), on weekends and in the season a swarm of small
old boats flies from the station to the greenhouse planets — **дачники**, grandmothers with carts
on engines among them. A hail: «Сынок, подбрось до теплиц» — a passenger job that pays in **jam**
(a thing in ВЕЩИ that the canteen takes for a meal) and a rumour. In the life document's lane they
are the traffic that makes a Saturday feel like a Saturday.

### Д10 The warmth rule — every joke has its earnest beat
The show's lesson, made a rule of design: **each satirical system ships with one sincere moment
that costs the player something or gives without being asked.** The ПАЛАТА (base §40) has its
clerk who keeps a parcel a day longer; the subscription firm has the engineer who unlocks your
lapsed shield for free after a rescue «между нами»; the named drone waits on the pad when you land;
the дачница gives jam; the hologram, on the free tariff, once skips the advert when your base is
burning. The satire is aimed at the state and the firm; the people are decent — exactly §7.1.

## 3. The queue — Д1–Д10, each playable on /dev

- **Д1 names and quirks for machines** — the table, the journal lines in the machine's name.
- **Д2 изолента** — the consumable, the field repair to 50 %, the tape drawn on the hull, the
  «кулибин» trait.
- **Д3 подписка** — parts and base modules by subscription at Компания and Хай-Фронт; the lapse at
  a сводка boundary announced in ПОЧТА; «экстренное продление ×3».
- **Д4 the AI core as a Хай-Фронт product** — the fourth-seat manager with three tariffs.
- **Д5 the naming rule** — toponym + the owner's suffix for settlements, holdings, metro stops;
  repainting on a flag change; invented firms.
- **Д6 the region-places** — with life Ж6.
- **Д7 the contradicting newscast** — the retelling answers the player's last deed per power;
  lands with P12 (ЭФИР).
- **Д8 Космопочта** — hours on the door, извещения, the counter, 30 days, the kind clerk.
- **Д9 дачники** — the weekend flow, the hail, the jam.
- **Д10 the warmth rule** — a checklist item for every satirical milestone, and the six beats above.

Cheapest first and most in the show's spirit: **Д1, Д2, Д5** (tables and lines, a day each), then
Д7 and Д8 (they ride the news and the desk), then the systems Д3/Д4.
