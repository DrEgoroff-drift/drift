# Review of the six designs of 14.09 — the critique, the reinvention, the humour, the look

The author, 14.09.2026, after the six documents: «смотри, чё придумали — критикуй, перепридумай, надо
юмор. Ещё дизайн этого всего придумай». This is the hostile pass over my own drafts (the rule:
one's own work is read meaner than anyone else's), what changes, the humour ledger and the art
direction. Where this contradicts a design document, **this wins**; each document carries a line
pointing here. Nothing is built.

## 1. The critique — seven faults that run through all six

1. **Six of everything.** Six posts, six hotels, six vestibules, six station bodies, six musical
   modes, six yard characters, six ways to run a railway. That is thirty-odd drawn things before
   one player sees a border. **Law now:** one body, six dressings. A dressing is exactly four
   things — a ground colour, a lettering, one prop, one light's motion — and nothing else may
   differ. Anything that needs a second body is cut.
2. **New screens.** The vestibule, the scheme, the КБ editor, the КНИЖКА page — the phone playtest
   of 13.09 says every screen must answer «чтобы что?» and the author asked for fewer, not more.
   **Law now:** two new screens in the whole batch — the vestibule paper and the КБ blueprint.
   Everything else lives in the world or on the pad.
3. **Decks on a phone are hidden state.** Three tabs of the same silhouette: what is on deck 2 is
   invisible while you look at deck 1, and both source games' first complaint was tedium — I
   tripled it. **Cut the deck tabs.** One plan view; depth is the ring of the silhouette (rim =
   обшивка, interior = палуба), read at a glance. БАШНЯ is a spine cell. Remember Tomorrow's arc
   rule survives (rim guns aim outward, spine 360°); its five floors do not.
4. **The «train» was undefined.** A hyperdrive into a ring is a lane; a lane has no interval, so
   ТАБЛО and the wait were fiction. **Now: the train is the batch.** The gate opens on its interval
   and everyone on the glide path goes together — you see them lined up on the lights ahead of you,
   a вахтовка, a barge, a yacht. Waiting has a picture; «people in the carriage» costs nothing.
5. **Penalties are not humour.** Tickets, trust ratings, voided warranties, baggage fees — half the
   «character» was a bill with no voice. A fee is funny only when the paper is; otherwise it is a
   grind. **Each fee keeps its voice or goes** (ledger §3); the trust rating that drops per shot
   is cut — it is EVE's sec status, not a joke.
6. **Humour was unevenly poured.** The birchpunk and the stamp have it; the shipyard, the
   resources and half of the life document are checklists. **Law now:** every system ships one
   running gag (repeatable, inside the world, never a reference) and one kindness (§3).
7. **Three things that would «бесить»**, in the author's word: the two-second alignment, the wait
   at the rim, a subscription dying mid-fight. Each gets a fail-safe with a voice: the ring pulls
   you in after 5 s («Автостыковка. Просьба не мешать»); the rim's табло never exceeds 40 s of
   real wait; the lapse always offers ×3 on the pad. Stated here so nobody argues later.

Per document, the verdict:

| document | keep | cut | change |
|---|---|---|---|
| borders | the stamp, the approach entry, station body by builder | six musical modes (→ a three-note motif per power on the radio), the trust rating, the law-of-the-land table as a system | the post is demoted to a dressing; the **first ship's gesture** (§2.1) becomes M452 |
| shipyard | cells, packer, fixpoint, yard characters, СТАПЕЛЬ, scars | deck tabs, «по разнарядке» as a restriction on the home power | one plan view; ГЛАВТРАССА's limit becomes a gag (the slogan) |
| life | the approach, billboards with prices, the food barge, hotels as doors | the giants (deferred to a later queue), six hotel bodies | the bazaar sells back what you threw away (§2.3) |
| metro | everything of the third draft | the coupling, six vestibule bodies | the batch as the train; alignment fail-safe; the announcements (§3) |
| resources | the ten, the tail, the reading | — | names lean to the register (§2.5); the eaters get voices |
| birchpunk | M485, M486, M489, M491, M492, M495 | farm, bath, kombucha, blockade, van → «base, deferred» (the base has fifty sections already) | the triangle is the centre |

## 2. The reinvention — what is different now

### 2.1 Borders: the first ship's gesture (new M452)
Within five seconds of a jump one ship does one characteristic thing to you. Cheaper than six
architectures and it *is* character, because character is behaviour:

| power | the gesture | what it leaves |
|---|---|---|
| ГЛАВТРАССА | a picket slides alongside, a spotlight cone sweeps your hull, «Записываю» | a line in КНИЖКА, literally: «Проследовал. Замечаний нет.» |
| Компания | a drone with a screen parks in front of your nose: «Добро пожаловать, партнёр!» and a bill | ПОЧТА: «Пролёт — 0 кр (акция). Сбор за оформление акции — 40 кр» |
| Орднунг | a scan plane passes down your hull, tail to nose | a form on the pad: purpose of visit — three answers, all «служебная» |
| Коммуна | nothing comes. A buoy with a hand-written board «ОБЕД. ВЕРНУСЬ» | — (the absence is the gesture) |
| Рассвет | a tug hails «чинить есть что?»; at 100 % hull: «ну хоть покрась» | a paint job for 5 кр, the tug's colour on one panel |
| Хай-Фронт | a camera drone falls in at a fixed offset and follows you to the dock | a line: «Ваш рейтинг доверия рассчитан» — and the rating is never shown anywhere |

The post stays as one construction with six dressings (§4.2), placed at the same entry point;
it is background, the gesture is the scene. The stamp (M453) is unchanged and is the best thing in
the batch.

### 2.2 Shipyard: one view, one document
The plan is **one blueprint**, nose up. Rim cells (the outer ring of the silhouette) are обшивка —
mounts; interior cells are палуба — everything else; the axis cells behind the nose third are the
spine — БАШНЯ. The player never switches a tab. The yard characters stay; two are rewritten:
- ГЛАВТРАССА: every hull it builds carries **a slogan along the flank that cannot be removed**
  («ПЛАН — ЗАКОН»; the slogan changes per сводка on their own ships, yours keeps the one it was
  built with). Its limit is no longer a restriction, it is a sentence you wear.
- Хай-Фронт: the hull's **firmware updates once a сводка and moves one part by one cell**,
  «оптимизировано»; the yard cannot switch it off, only «отложить обновление» for 3 сводки.
The blueprint is a document: it is **«согласовано»** by a stamp that lands by itself after a short
fake queue («ваш чертёж 4-й в очереди»), and ТИПОВОЙ is labelled **«КАК У ВСЕХ»**.

### 2.3 Life: the bazaar remembers
The bazaar of hulks (M463) sells back **what you threw away**. Every part discarded in ОПИСЬ goes
into a small ring buffer (`G.thrown`, twelve entries, the only new save field in the life batch)
and turns up on a stall within a few сводки at ×3, with a tag «ношеная, один хозяин». Nothing else
in the life document is a mechanic; this one is, and it is the joke and the economy at once.
The hotels keep one body: a slab of windows; the dressing changes the sign and which windows are
lit (ГЛАВТРАССА a hundred windows and two dead letters; Компания every window lit and a fountain
of light; Орднунг lights out at 22:00 by the game clock; Коммуна half the windows, a terrace;
Рассвет a door cut into rock; Хай-Фронт a honeycomb, one cell flickering — the *one* permitted
flicker in the batch, see §4.1).

### 2.4 Metro: the batch and the fail-safes
- The **train is the batch**: ships that arrived on the glide path since the last opening stand
  in a row on the lights; on «поезд подан» they go in one after another, you among them, 0.3 s apart.
  At the rim the batch is you and one drone.
- Alignment: two seconds in the cone, assisted; after 5 s of failing the ring takes you anyway —
  «Автостыковка. Просьба не мешать» — and a line in КНИЖКА: «стыковка выполнена автоматикой».
- Wait: the табло's next departure is real game time capped at 40 s of real wait at the rim, ~6 s
  in the heart. The buffet is what you do meanwhile.
- The vestibule paper is the **only** new screen of the metro; the scheme opens *inside* it.

### 2.5 Resources: names in the register, eaters with voices
Гелий-3 → **«солнечный газ»**; палладий → **«белая руда»**; осмий stays (it is a word people
know for «heaviest»); магнитная пыль, жемчуг пустоты, тёмное стекло, звёздный чернозём, янтарь,
нейтронная крошка stay; антивещество в ловушке → **«ловушки»** on the hold row, the full name on
the card. Each eater speaks on its counter (ledger §3).

## 3. The humour ledger — one running gag and one kindness per system

The law: the gag is the world's own logic taken seriously (base §40); it repeats; it never winks.
The kindness is a person being decent inside the institution (§7.1).

| system | running gag | kindness |
|---|---|---|
| **borders** | the stamp; Компания's «0 кр (акция) + 40 кр за оформление акции»; Орднунг's three answers, all «служебная»; ГЛАВТРАССА's picket writes «Замечаний нет» every single time, and once a month «Замечание: нет замечаний» | Рассвет's tug paints one panel for 5 кр and says which colour suits the hull |
| **shipyard** | «согласовано» after a fake queue; the slogan you cannot remove; Компания's receipt per moved cell («перемещение ячейки — 1 кр, итого 14 кр, спасибо за выбор»); Орднунг refuses a turned footprint — «поворот не предусмотрен формуляром»; Коммуна adds a curve you did not order — «так красивее»; Хай-Фронт's firmware moves a part — «оптимизировано»; ТИПОВОЙ = «КАК У ВСЕХ» | the old master at any yard welds one seam for nothing if your hull carries tape: «сынок, ну кто ж так» |

| **metro** | the announcer: «Осторожно, двери закрываются» — a ship has no doors, and it is said anyway; «Уступайте места пассажирам с детьми и крупногабаритным грузом»; «Поезд следует до станции «Край» со всеми остановками. ## 4. The look — art direction for the whole batch

### 4.1 One language, four rules
- **Cold key, warm accent.** Space and structures stay in the cold greys and blues the game already
  has; neon, stamps, ink and the ЖИЛА word are the warm accents. Neon is never the key light — the
  star is.
- **A tube is three strokes.** Every neon line is drawn as a wide low-alpha glow of its colour, a
  medium core, and a thin near-white centre — the «стопка слоёв малой альфы» rule. A dead letter is
  the medium core alone, dim grey, no glow. **No flicker anywhere** (motion, not twinkle) except one
  Хай-Фронт honeycomb cell per hotel and one dying ГЛАВТРАССА letter that *buzzes* once a minute
  (a 200 ms dip), which is a moment, not a twinkle.
- **Painted once.** Posts, billboards, hotels, gate rings, buoys are sprites baked per system;
  what moves per frame is the chasing lights (a phase), the crawling text, the ships. `prof()` on
  the phone layout after every pass.
- **Body, outline, one light** for every new thing — the craft codex judges them and neon gets its
  own almanac issue.

### 4.2 The dressing — what may differ between powers, and nothing else

| | ground | lettering | prop | light's motion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ГЛАВТРАССА | санкирь + grey | red stencil, ГОСТ-like, one letter dead | a red star / a slogan board | two amber beacons turning |
| Компания | white + one blue stripe | rounded sans, a ™ | a ring logo, a running line | the running line, left to right |
| Орднунг | grey steel, black ribs | numbered, condensed | a numbered plate | a scan plane sweeping |
| Коммуна | blue and white | italic, a flourish | a garland, a chair | a slow warm pulse (breathing) |
| Рассвет | ochre and black | hand-painted, uneven | a painted sun | the sun turning slowly |
| Хай-Фронт | white | dot-matrix, a version string | a red dot, an antenna | light from under the plating, one eye that turns to you |

Everything in §5's drawings — post, hotel, vestibule, gate ring — is one body wearing one row of
this table.

### 4.3 The phone frame
Portrait 390×844, nose up. The approach runs from the bottom of the screen to the station: the
player's eye already travels that line. The gate ring sits past the station, top of the frame at
zoom 1. Pads and prompts as today (the verb from the prompt). Nothing new hangs over the world
except the alignment cone while docking.

### 4.4 The drawings, one by one
- **The gesture ship** (M452): the fleet art's silhouette with the maker's dressing; the spotlight is
  a cone of the cold key at low alpha with its warm edge on your hull; the scan plane is a single
  line the width of the frame passing once.
- **The post** (background): a truss with a board, one lamp, the dressing's prop. Baked; only the
  lamp's motion per frame.
- **The stamp** (M453): DOM on the КНИЖКА paper, tilted 5–12° by seed, ink with alpha grain, slams in
  at scale 1.3 → 1 in 120 ms, holds 900 ms, fades. Violet for ГЛАВТРАССА, black for Орднунг, blue
  ink for Коммуна, ochre for Рассвет, dot-matrix grey for Хай-Фронт; Компания's is a till slip
  that scrolls up from the bottom edge instead of stamping.
- **The lane** (M459): buoys as small dark bodies with one lamp each; the lamps' phase chases toward
  the dock at ~2 buoys/s. The queue: ships on a slow ellipse at the dock, one peeling off per
  minute, one joining.
- **Billboards** (M460): a truss, a panel, three-stroke neon lettering, one crawling line in the
  station's paper font. Within R of a billboard the hull gets an additive glow stamp of the
  panel's colour on the facing side (the warm accent on the body).
- **Hotels** (M461): one slab body, a grid of windows lit per the dressing, a three-stroke sign above.
- **The gate ring** (M471): seen from above — a torus lying flat, its inner disc a shade lighter than
  space with a faint slow spiral (the drive); the glide path two converging dotted lines of lamps
  chasing inward. Departure: the ship's stars stretch toward the ring's centre for 0.6 s, a
  white-cyan flash, gone. The batch stands on the lamps in a row.
- **The vestibule paper** (M472): the station screens' cream paper, one page. ТАБЛО is a **split-flap
  board** — flaps turn when a line changes (motion with a reason; the Aeroflot-hall image). КАССА a
  window with a жетон drawn as a brass disc; БУФЕТ three items on a card with prices; КУДА ВАМ a
  wide button that unfolds the scheme on the same paper.
- **The scheme**: the Moscow scheme of the seventies — thick coloured lines (colour is allowed on
  paper, as every metro scheme allows it), white circles with black rims for stops, a double
  circle for interchanges, Cyrillic sans, «ВЫ ЗДЕСЬ» in red. On the map beneath, the lines are
  faint smooth curves 1:1 with the sheet.
- **The ride** (M473): the galaxy map with the line ahead drawn thick in the scheme's colour; the
  train is a small rounded glyph with a headlight wedge; a stop is the glyph halting and a ring
  expanding once; at the top a paper strip like the carriage's running line carries the
  announcement; ВЫЙТИ on the pad in the frame's own hierarchy.
- **The blueprint** (M477): **синька** — deep Prussian-blue paper, the silhouette and grid in
  light-blue/white line, the parts as **warm ochre ink stamps** by kind (gun a barrel glyph, reactor
  a circle with a dot, tank a rounded bar, hold a hatch), БАШНЯ a circle with a cross through the
  spine, a violet «СОГЛАСОВАНО» in a corner, scars as brown burn marks, tape as a grey strip. Nose
  up, the plan fills the width, the tray under it. The «Техника — молодёжи» centrefold, not a
  spreadsheet.
- **The resources** (M465–M469): ten glyphs in the ТРЮМ, hues spread round the wheel so the hold stays a
  rich palette: солнечный газ pale gold; белая руда bone; янтарь amber with a dark fleck; осмий
  blue-black, matte, one hard highlight; чернозём near-black brown; магнитная пыль violet-grey;
  жемчуг a cold white with a faint rainbow rim; тёмное стекло obsidian with one reflection;
  ловушки a black cell with a thin violet ring that thins as the countdown runs; крошка the
  heaviest grey, drawn small — five cells wide in the hold but a tiny thing in the picture.
- **ЖИЛА**: the word across the screen in the ГЛАВТРАССА stencil, warm, 1.2 s, the only time the
  game shouts.

## 5. Order, revised

M452 (the gesture) + M453 (the stamp) + M459 (the approach) in one pass — they share the entry point and
answer «чья земля» in five seconds with the least drawing. Then M465–M467, then M470–M473 with the batch,
then M476–M478 with the single-view blueprint, then M485/M486/M495. The giants, the base-side birchpunk and
the six musical modes are out of this batch.
