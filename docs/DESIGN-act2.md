# The second act — the expedition (M154–M158)

Design, 2026-08-23. Talked through with the author; the three forks at the end were taken by the
assistant under the standing "run the plan" order and can be reversed before M154 is built.

## Why a second act

The hundred stories, fifteen regions, rumours, returners, passports and the receiver make a world
that *speaks*. What it lacks is an event that changes the world itself, not only what the player
notices in it. Soviet space fiction is built on exactly that: not an enemy, but a **big common
undertaking** and the price people pay for it (Efremov, the Strugatskys' "Trainees" and "Far
Rainbow", Bulychev's "The Settlement", institute fiction). The periphery we built is a world living
*after* one such push. The second act is the push happening again.

## Spine: the expedition beyond the edge of the Drift

1. **The circular.** One day the ether carries a circular: an expedition is being fitted out to go
   beyond the edge. From that day every region works for it. Counter queues change ("we are
   collecting for the expedition"), prices creep, barges leave with people aboard, settlements
   "give" a person each (a house glyph goes dark, a line in the settlement speech).
2. **The player is not the hero.** The player is one of those who *fit it out*: carries tapes,
   parts, letters, a person now and then. No quest log; the demand shows through the existing
   channels (queue, table, rumours, receiver, the plan of `11r-plan`).
3. **The six rivals are people on this spine.** Each leaves a trace in another region; the traces
   together make one route that leads to the sixth — the one who has no face yet. The sixth is the
   one who goes with the expedition. Closes the M131 "links as data" tail.
4. **The misclosure is the unexplained third.** The expedition goes where the numbers do not add
   up: in one region the sky's count and the people's count diverge, recorders from different
   stations disagree, the hours drift (M136-hours already exists). Tapes laid on the table
   together show a figure. Nobody explains it.
5. **The departure.** The expedition leaves. The board of returners gets a line without a name.

## Forks — decisions taken (reversible)

- **Does it return?** No. A year later a single tape arrives, unsigned; the board keeps the
  nameless line. (The "Far Rainbow" key, not the happy one.)
- **Can the player go with it?** Yes, and it is an ending — optional, offered once, at the
  departure. Going ends the game with the log closed at that line.
- **The Tin.** It closes: "the last run". Each region sends one person to it; the player may or
  may not be in time with somebody's letter.

## Milestones

- **M154 circular** — `11x-expedition`: the circular, the world's demand through queues, prices,
  barges, settlements; persisted `G.exp`.
- **M155 the sixth** — the rival route as links in data; the sixth gets a face and a story.
- **M156 misclosure** — the region where the counts diverge; recorder tapes as a figure on the table.
- **M157 last run** — the Tin closes; letters with content (the postal round has envelopes, not
  letters — ten letters the player carries and does not read; the addressee reads aloud).
- **M158 departure** — the board line, the optional ending, the unsigned tape a year later.

Kept for after the act: the table as paper and the removal of the overlay HUD (release look), so
the interface is made for the act and not before it.
